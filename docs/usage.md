# Using Ask Away!

## The four pages of an event

Every event has a 6-character code, e.g. `K7F2Q9`.

| Page | Address | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Participant page** | `/e/K7F2Q9` | Your audience on their phones: ask, vote, see answers |
| **Presenter view** | `/e/K7F2Q9/present` | The computer connected to the projector |
| **Moderation** | `/e/K7F2Q9/admin` | You and your moderators |
| **Link preview** | — | When you share the participant link in a chat, it shows the event title |

## Before the event

1. On the start page, enter a title and click **Let's go**. Under *More options*
   you can add a description, a date and whether questions need approval.
2. You land on the **moderation page**. It's saved in your browser.
   - **Log in** (just your email, no password) and use *Share → Add to my account*
     so you can open it from any device. You need this for the newsletter sign-up.
3. Under **Share** you find:
   - the **participant link** for everyone,
   - the **presenter view** link for the projector computer,
   - a **moderator link** for helpers. They can moderate and switch the screen,
     but can't change settings or delete the event. You can replace this link
     at any time; the old one stops working immediately.
   - your **owner link**. Keep it private: it gives full control.
4. Under **Settings**, check *"New questions need approval before they appear"*:
   - **On**: every question waits under *Waiting* until a moderator accepts it.
     Recommended for public or political events.
   - **Off**: questions appear immediately. You can still hide or delete them.

## During the event

**On the projector**, open the presenter view and press **F** for fullscreen.

Use the **"On the big screen"** switch on the moderation page (or press **R** / **Q**
on the projector computer) to choose what is shown:

- **Join screen**: big QR code, the link and the event code, plus how many people
  are connected. Show this at the beginning so people can join.
- **Questions**: the top questions (pinned first, then most votes), updated live.
  A small QR code stays in the corner for latecomers.

**Guided tours**: the first time you open the moderation page, a short tour
shows the most important controls. Each section (*Waiting*, *Live*, *Share*,
*Settings*) has its own mini-tour that runs the first time you open it, so
moderators who only approve questions never see the rest. Replay any tour via
**Help** (top right). To stop tours from starting on their own, click *Don't
show tips automatically* in any tour or in the Help menu. This is remembered in
your browser for all events and can be switched back on in the same menu.

**Moderating** (tabs on the moderation page):

- **Waiting**: accept ✅ or decline ✖️ new questions.
- **Live**: the questions as shown on screen.
  - **Spotlight** 🎤 puts a question big on the projector ("Now answering") and
    highlights it on everyone's phone.
  - **Answered** ✅ moves it out of the list (also ends the spotlight).
  - **Pin** ⭐ keeps it at the top regardless of votes.
  - **Hide** moves it to *Declined*.
- **Answered** / **Declined**: you can move questions back from here.

**Participants** see a short welcome on their first visit: how to ask, that
they can upvote with 👍, and whether questions are checked by a moderator first.
They can pick a name and animal right there or stay anonymous with a random
animal name (e.g. "Anonymous Otter"). *How it works* below the question field
shows the welcome again.
They can change it or add their own name. They can edit or withdraw their own
questions while those are waiting for approval.

**Spam protection**: each person can ask one question every 30 seconds (adjustable
by the host), and votes are rate-limited. The messages for this are friendly. 😮‍💨

## After the event

1. **Settings → Close event**: no more questions or votes. Participants see a
   thank-you screen, and the projector shows "That's a wrap!". You can reopen it.
2. **Export** the questions as CSV (Excel) or Markdown.
3. If you enabled the **stay-in-touch sign-up**, export the sign-ups (CSV) and
   then delete them from Ask Away! (see below).
4. The event and everything in it is **deleted automatically** after the
   retention period (30 days by default, set by the host). You can also delete it
   right away.

## Stay-in-touch sign-up (newsletter)

Available for events that belong to an account, if the host has set up email.

1. **Settings → Stay-in-touch sign-up**: enter who sends the updates (e.g.
   "Anna Rosenwasser"), what people will get, and optionally a link to your privacy
   policy. Switch it on.
2. When you close the event, participants see a short form on their phones. The
   consent text is generated from what you entered, and the exact wording is
   stored with each sign-up.
3. People get an email to **confirm** (double opt-in). Only confirmed sign-ups are
   exported. Unconfirmed ones are deleted after 7 days.

**Data protection notes:**

- **You are responsible** for the sign-ups and how they are used, not the instance
  host. Name yourself or your organisation clearly as the sender.
- Signing up for a newsletter at a **political event** can reveal political
  opinions. Under Swiss data protection law (nDSG) and the GDPR these count as
  especially sensitive data, which is why consent must be explicit and specific.
  Ask Away! never pre-ticks the box and stores the consent text and time.
- Only email and an optional name are collected. Sign-ups are **not linked** to
  questions or votes.
- Export soon after the event, import into your newsletter tool (which handles
  unsubscribing), then use **Delete sign-ups**.

## Accounts

Logging in needs no password: enter your email and you get a 6-digit code.
Accounts let you find your events on any device (**My events**), add events
created anonymously to your account, and use the newsletter sign-up.

Depending on how the host configured the instance, creating events may require an
account, and new accounts may be invite-only.
